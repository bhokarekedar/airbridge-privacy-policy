Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Airbridge: Airtable Sync ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you install and use our Shopify application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

When you install the App, we automatically access certain types of information from your Shopify store in order to provide data synchronization services to your designated Airtable account:

  • Store Information: Shop domain, access tokens, and store configuration details required to authenticate with the Shopify API.
  • Product & Inventory Data: Product titles, descriptions, prices, SKUs, barcode numbers, inventory quantities, and product tags.
  • Order Data: Order IDs, creation dates, order status, financial status, line items, and fulfillment status.
  • Customer Data: First and last name, email address, phone number, and customer tags (collected solely to populate customer and order records in your connected Airtable base). We do not collect or store full physical addresses.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal and store data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To synchronize product, inventory, order, and customer records between Shopify and your Airtable workspace.
  • To process automated background scheduled sync tasks (e.g., 15-minute, hourly, or daily sync intervals).
  • To provide you with sync history logs, error diagnostics, and in-app support.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or store data to third parties, nor do we use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take data protection and privacy seriously:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between Shopify, our servers, and Airtable is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive credentials (such as Airtable Personal Access Tokens and session tokens) are securely stored and encrypted at rest.
  • Data Minimization: We only store the minimal metadata required for session authentication and sync logs. The primary destination of your synced data is your own Airtable workspace.

4. Shopify GDPR Mandatory Webhooks

Airbridge complies fully with Shopify's mandatory data privacy webhooks:

  • Customer Data Request (customers/data_request): When requested by a merchant on behalf of a customer, we facilitate exporting any customer data processed.
  • Customer Data Erasure (customers/redact): When a customer requests deletion of their personal data, we delete any cached customer records from our logs within 30 days.
  • Shop Redact (shop/redact): Within 48 hours of an app uninstallation, all store data, access tokens, and configuration logs associated with the shop are permanently purged.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates directly with Airtable (Formagrid, Inc.) via their official REST API. Please review Airtable's Privacy Policy to understand how Airtable manages data stored in your workspace.

6. Merchant and Customer Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR and CCPA), you and your customers have the right to access, rectify, or delete personal information held by us. You can exercise these rights by uninstalling the application or contacting us directly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. We will notify merchants of any material changes via app updates or email notices.
